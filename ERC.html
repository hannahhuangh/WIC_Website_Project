Eleanor Roosevelt College

Scholarship, Leadership and Service

Theme Description

Eleanor Roosevelt College’s theme is Global Citizenship, World Cultures, and International Understanding. ERC emphasizes cross-cultural awareness, global interconnectedness, and preparing students to engage with worldwide challenges such as inequality, conflict, migration, and cultural diversity. The college encourages students to understand their role as global citizens through academic study, community engagement, and intercultural experiences such as studying abroad or living in I-House.

  • Natural Sciences (2 courses)
  • Quantitative/Formal Skills (2 courses)
  • Fine Arts (1 course)
  • Regional Specialization (3 courses)
  • Language Other Than English (proficiency through the 4th quarter)
Housing

ERC is located on the northwestern edge of campus, offering ocean views and a vibrant residential community influenced by global engagement through International House (I-House). First-year students typically live in Asante Hall or Africa Hall, which offer suite-style living with shared lounges. Upperclassmen often move into ERC Apartments which include kitchens, living rooms, and more independence.

Dining

ERC’s primary dining space is Café Ventanas, known for its large outdoor seating, ocean views, and rotating global cuisine reflecting ERC’s international focus. Ventanas offers vegetarian, vegan, halal, and culturally themed menus.
