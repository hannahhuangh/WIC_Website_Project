Muir College

Celebrating the Independent Spirit

Theme Description

Muir College’s theme is centered around environmental awareness, intellectual growth, community responsibility, and independent spirit. Inspired by naturalist John Muir, the college encourages students to think critically, make informed choices, and engage in the responsibility of their community and environment. The college’s commitment to environmentalism is reflected through its efforts in establishing community-focused programs and sustainability initiatives across the entire campus. Muir College cultivates a supportive environment that celebrates individuality, learning, and social engagement.

General Education Requirements

Muir’s flexible GE requirements heavily emphasize flexibility, allowing students to tailor their individual academic paths, promoting both intellectual freedom and curiosity on a liberal arts foundation. For the writing requirement, Muir students must complete a two-course Muir College Writing Program (MCWP 40 and MCWP 50), which focuses on critical thinking, rhetorical analysis, and expository writing.

This college also offers the Muir Special Project major, which is a Bachelor of Arts degree following 2 specific paths. It can be an interdisciplinary major that consists of 48 upper-divisions united from 2 or more departments designed to form a course of study comparable to a traditional major. It can also be an independent project that focuses on making a piece of creative work such as a research paper, art installation, etc.

Breadth Requirements

Students must complete:

  • One 3-quarter sequence in Social Science
  • One 3-quarter sequence in Math or Natural Science
  • Two additional 3-quarter sequences in Fine Arts, Humanities, or Foreign Language

Housing

Muir College students live relatively central to campus. They are located near one of the Gymnasiums on campus, and also right across from the Sun God lawn. A decent selection of classes and buildings around Muir are focused on Mathematics, Biology, and also a variety of social sciences. Muir College students live in close-knit communities.

There are two main residence halls–Tioga and Tenaya–which mainly house first years. These two buildings feature suites of single rooms, double rooms, and triple rooms often housing between 4-6 residents. Each residential suite has a common lounge, restrooms, and showers. Muir also offers apartment style living–Tamarack and Tuolumne Apartments–which house 4-6 residents and also offers a kitchen in each suite. Laundry rooms are available in each building.

Dining

Muir has its very own dining hall called Pines. Pines offers a variety of options from salads, poke, vegetarian meals, desserts, etc.

In addition to the dining hall, Muir also has its little market called Johns Market that offers quick snacks, drinks, and a limited amount of produce. Most importantly, however, is Moms cafe! Moms cafe was a student organized initiative that has turned into a favorite study sport for many students at UCSD. They offer a variety of drinks and very cozy seating for students to come in, relax, and get work done with friends.
