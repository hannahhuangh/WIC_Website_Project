Sixth College

Description

The theme of sixth college is Culture, Art, and Technology and it aims to prepare students for the challenges through creativity, collaboration, and interdisciplinary learning. It emphasizes flexible thinking, creativity, adaptability to technological changes, and the ability to engage with a wide range of backgrounds. Students learn to communicate and research using both digital and traditional tools while engaging in community-based activities. And through this integrated academic and experiential learning, students are able to connect their learning with their residential and communities.

GE Requirements

Full List

Culture, Art, and Technology(CAT)

  • Three-quarter core sequence(CAT 1, 2, and 3)
  • Upper-division writing: studying public rhetoric and practical communication
    • CAT 125 or CAT 125R(remote)
    • Equivalent Global seminar(study abroad program)
  • Experimental Learning(4-unit): special courses, internship, research

Information Technology Fluency(1 programming course)

  • Can be fulfilled by a score of 4 or 5 on AP CSA

Social Analysis (2 social sciences course / 8 units)

  • Can be fulfilled by a score of 4 or 5 on AP Economics, Government, or Psychology

Narrative, Aestehtic, and Historical Reasoning (2 humanities courses / 8 units)

  • Can be fulfilled by a score of 3, 4, or 5 on: AP Art History, History, Foreign Language, or Literature(non-English)

Analytic Methodologies/Scientific Method (2 natural science courses / 8 units)

  • Can be fulfilled by a score of:
    • 3, 4, or 5 on AP Biology, Chemistry, or Physics 1, 2, or C
    • 4 or 5 on AP Environmental Science

Structured Reasoning (1 Math/logic course / 4 units)

  • Can be fulfilled by a score of 3, 4, or 5 on AP Statistics

Art Making (8 units)

  • Can be fulfilled by a score of 3, 4, or 5 on AP Art Studio or music

Housing Layout

Sixth college is near the central campus, making it convenient for students to get to most of the places on campus. Housing styles are mostly suite-style, and first-year students generally live in double and triple rooms(with some singles) in three of four buildings, Catalyst, Kaleidoscope, and Tapestry, where Mosaic has apartment-style housing. Each floor has a shared suite lounge and kitchen for students to study and socialize.

Dining Hall

Sixth College offers a wide variety of food options, including plant-based entrées, poke bowls, noodles, global street food, BBQ, burritos, nachos, and more. The Sixth Market also features its own coffeehouse, where you can enjoy pastries, sweets, and drinks. The market is open from 7 a.m. to 2 a.m. Monday through Thursday, and until 11 p.m. on other days. Around the neighborhood, there are several restaurants that accept Triton Cash, such as Fan Fan, a Chinese restaurant known for its delicious boba and malatang that offers from 9–11 p.m. Monday through Thursdays; Showa Ramen, which serves Japanese ramen and chicken potstickers; and Blue Bowl, students’ favorite spot for acai bowls.
