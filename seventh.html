Seventh College

A Changing Planet

Theme Description

Seventh College’s theme is “A Changing Planet”. The college focuses on climate change, sustainability, migration, and the societal impacts of rapid environmental and technological shifts. The curriculum emphasizes interdisciplinary problem solving, community engagement, and preparing students to address real-world ecological and social challenges.

General Education Requirements
  • Synthesis: two courses of university-level writing and one project-based course
  • High-impact experience: internship, mentored research, or study abroad
  • Art: 2 courses (AP credit may apply)
  • Humanities: 2 courses
  • Natural Sciences & Engineering: 2 courses
  • Quantitative Reasoning: 2 courses
  • Social Sciences: 2 courses

Housing

Seventh College housing is known for ocean views and proximity to RIMAC. Students live in a mix of residence halls and towers; many first-years live in shared rooms while upper-division students move into apartments or suite-style housing. Common lounges and study spaces are available across residence areas.

Dining

Seventh College dining includes a variety of high-quality options, including sushi and udon at Bistro, vegetarian and local-produce options, and desserts. Dining locations often offer diverse menus to suit different dietary needs.
