Seventh College

Description

Seventh College focuses on the theme “A Changing Planet” that helps students tackle global issues like climate change, migration, and technological shifts through an interdisciplinary approach. It provides a supportive and inclusive community and a flexible curriculum that builds real-world skills in communication, research, teamwork, and problem-solving, preparing students for the future.

Housing Layout

Seventh College is at a beautiful ocean-view location with stunning sunrises and sunsets. It’s right next to the RIMAC gym and the Rady School of Management. There’s also a game room and a common area called Northbreak where students can hang out or study together. Seventh has a variety of layouts, with many buildings and towers. The only downside is that it’s a bit far from the central campus, so students usually take the bus to get to other places on campus.

Dining Hall

Seventh College is known for its high-quality sushi and udon served at its restaurant, Bistro. The menu features a variety of Asian dishes, including fresh seafood, crisp greens, local produce, vegetarian options, noodles, and fried rice. It also offers drinks like oat milk boba tea and delicious desserts such as ice cream and mango sago. Since it’s a popular spot, it’s best to order early to avoid the crowds.
