Eighth College

A great place to live, learn, and thrive!

Theme Description

Eighth College's theme is Community & Engagement. As the newest college up to date, it focuses heavily on the heart of some of the most difficult challenges that humanity faces worldwide: structural racism, climate disruption, ecological degradation, etc. Additionally, Eighth College's theme aligns with the campus-wide efforts to combat structural racism and anti-Blackness.

General Education Requirements

Eighth Writing (CCE Series)

This is a four-course sequence that must be taken for a letter grade.

Prerequisite: Students must satisfy the UC Entry Level Writing Requirement (ELWR) before enrolling in CCE courses.

  • CCE 1
  • CCE 2
  • CCE 3
  • CCE 120

These courses explore community care, structural inequities, local partnerships, and collaborative learning across diverse backgrounds.

Breadth Requirements

In addition to the writing courses, students are also required to meet their breadth requirements. No matter their major/area of study, all students are required to complete:

  • 2 Arts courses
  • 2 Humanities courses
  • 2 Natural Science courses
  • 2 Quantitative Methods & Engineering courses
  • 2 Social Science courses

More information: UCSD Eighth College Breadth Requirement Courses

Housing

Eighth college students are lucky enough to be situated in the newest residential buildings on campus. Although situated on the southern point of campus, students have some of the best ocean views, and also have close access to the Amazon Hub and La Jolla playhouse. There are classrooms in the Podemos building of Eighth college, which are used for a variety of different courses. There are a total of 5 buildings: Pulse, Podemos, Sankofa, Azad, and Survivance. These buildings all consist of a mix of apartments (with kitchen) and residential halls (no kitchen). All suites will have their own bathrooms and showers and laundry rooms are offered in every building as well. Dining

Although the dining hall at Eighth is not yet completed, there is projected to be a new food hall called the Station8 Public Market consisting of 10 restaurants opening up. There is no expected date of opening yet. However, it will open across from a new Italian restaurant called Dora. The current closest dining hall to Eighth college is 64. 64 consists of a wide variety of cuisines ranging from American style cuisine, Asian, Italian, and more. There also exists a market in it, where you are able to use your dining dollars to purchase snacks, drinks, produce, and more.
