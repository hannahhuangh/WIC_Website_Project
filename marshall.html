Thurgood Marshall College

The student as scholar and citizen

Theme Description

Thurgood Marshall College’s theme is grounded in the belief that students are both scholars and citizens, emphasizing that academic excellence and social responsibility are equally essential. Founded on principles of diversity, equity-minded education, and community engagement, the college encourages students to understand and challenge the social, cultural, and structural contexts that shape everyday life.

Marshall College’s spirit of community—symbolized by its logo of three joined hands—reflects its commitment to collaboration, civic responsibility, and collective growth. The college offers exceptional support for transfer and international students, highlights artistic expression through its Artist in Residence Program, and maintains strong ties to local communities through initiatives such as the Public Service Minor, the Dimensions of Culture (DOC) program, and the Partnership Schools outreach program.

Housing

Thurgood Marshall College students now live in the newly developed Ridge Walk Living & Learning Neighborhood, a modern residential community located near the center of campus. The Ridge Walk apartments offer spacious living for first-years and continuing students alike, featuring fully furnished units with shared living rooms, kitchens, and multiple bedrooms. These buildings emphasize community and collaboration through open lounges, study areas, and outdoor gathering spaces. Upperclassmen benefit from the added independence provided by apartment-style living, while still being connected to the supportive, equity-driven community that characterizes Thurgood Marshall College.

Dining

Marshall College students enjoy access to two major dining locations: OceanView Terrace (OVT) and the newly opened Goody’s Marketplace in the Ridge Walk Living & Learning Neighborhood.

OceanView Terrace, located on the fifth floor overlooking the coast, offers a Mediterranean-inspired dining experience with stone-fired pizzas, pasta bakes, sandwiches, salads, and fresh-baked desserts. Students can enjoy a relaxed meal on the outdoor patio while taking in beautiful ocean views, making OVT a popular spot for both studying and socializing.

Goody’s Marketplace is designed as a vibrant student-centered hub featuring a diverse chicken-focused menu including hand-breaded sandwiches, tenders, salads, breakfast burritos, and globally inspired flavors. Goody’s will introduce UC San Diego’s first-ever self-serve barista station, offering freshly brewed coffee, espresso drinks, and iced beverages. The marketplace will also provide essential groceries and utilize the same contact-free checkout technology used at Roger’s Market and The Market at Seventh. A wide range of payment options—including Dining Dollars, Triton Cash, credit cards, and EBT—ensures accessibility for all students.
