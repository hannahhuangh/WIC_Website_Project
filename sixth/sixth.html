Sixth College

Culture, Art, and Technology

Theme Description

Sixth College’s theme is Culture, Art, and Technology (CAT). The college prepares students to think creatively and work across disciplines — combining arts, humanities, and technological practice to address complex problems. CAT emphasizes experimentation, digital fluency, and community-engaged learning so students can connect academic study with real-world cultural and technological contexts.

General Education Requirements

Core CAT Sequence

Students complete a multi-quarter CAT core sequence and additional requirements:

  • Three-quarter core sequence (CAT 1, CAT 2, CAT 3)
  • Upper-division writing: public rhetoric and practical communication (e.g., CAT 125 or CAT 125R)
  • Experimental learning (4-unit): internships, mentored research, or special projects
  • Information technology fluency: one programming or computing course (or AP CSA credit)
  • Social Analysis, Humanities, Natural Sciences, Structured Reasoning, and Art Making (see college site)

Full GE and credit policies: Sixth GE Requirements (PDF)

Housing

Sixth College housing is primarily suite-style. First-year students typically live in buildings like Catalyst, Kaleidoscope, and Tapestry (double/triple rooms with some singles). Mosaic offers apartment-style housing for upper-division students. Floors usually include a shared suite lounge and kitchen for study and socializing.

Dining

Sixth College dining features diverse options including plant-based entrées, bowls, global street food, and a coffeehouse. The Sixth Market serves a range of items and nearby neighborhood spots accept Triton Cash.
