Description

Warren College contiues U.S. Supreme Court Chief Justice Earl Warren's legacy. Warren college is the fourth establised undergraduate college in 1974. The College encourages students to explore the relationship between ethics, society, and the law, and is dedicated to cultivating ethically responsible citizen scholars

General Education Requierments

Warren College Writing Program

This is a two course sequence which must be taken for a letter grade at UCSD.

Prerequitite: You must satisfy the UC Entry Level Writing Requirement (ELWR) before enrolling in WCWP 10A. To fulfill ELWR student may meet requirnment through test scores (IB, AP, SAT, or ACT) or may meet requirenment by earning a "C" or better in better in a UC-transferable English composition course. Those who do not fulfill ELWR must complete complete UCSD's Writing Placement Process (WPP) in summer.

  • WCWP 10A
  • WCWP 10B

Ethics and Society (2 courses)

This is a two course sequence which must be taken for a letter grade at UCSD. You may choose to take one class from each department or both classes from the same department.

Prerequitite: Must complete Warren Writing (WCWP 10A-B) before enrolling.

  • PHIL 27 or POLI 27
  • PHIL 28 or POLI 28

Programs of Concentration (PofCs) or Area Studies

Two Programs of Concentration (PofCs) or Area Studies outside of your major discipline are required.

Area Studies - B.S. Engineering Majors ONLY

Two Area Studies are Required:

  • Each must contain three four-unit courses, two of which must be upper-division
  • Must be non-contiguous (unrelated) to the major and to each other
  • At least one course from each Area Study must be taken at UC San Diego
  • Choose one Area Study from each of the two disciplines listed below
    • Humanities and Fine Arts
    • Social Sciences

    Programs of Concentration (PofCs) - majors except B.S engineering majors

    Two Programs of Concentration (PofCs) are Required:

  • Each must contain six four-unit courses, three of which must be upper-division (numbered 100+) unless noted
  • Must be non-contiguous (unrelated) to the major and to each other
  • At least two courses from each PofC must be taken at UC San Diego
  • Choose one Area Study from each of the two disciplines listed below
    • Humanities and Fine Arts
    • Social Sciences
    • Math, Natural Sciences, and Physical Sciences
    Housing

    There are two types of living spaces in Warren. Apartments or Residential Halls

    Residential Halls: Only first year students are perminted to live in the Warren Residential halls.

    • Frankfurter
    • Harlan
    • Stewart

    All fully furnished and a common area lunge, community bathroom, and six rooms - 4 singles and 2 triples. Each student will have a bed, dresser, and desk with chair. The common lounge will have a coffee table, two couches, and a side table.

    Apartments: Continuing Students and some first year students can live in Warren Apartments

    • Black
    • Brennan
    • Douglas
    • Goldberg
    • Bates and Brown: Only continuing students and tansfer students live in these apartments

    All fully furnished and a kitchen, common lounge, and community bathroom. Apartments hold a total of 4 - 6 students, double double (for Brennan, Black, and Goldberg), double-triple (Douglas), 4 singles for Bates and Brown. Living room has a couch, coffee table, and a kitchen table with chairs. THe kitchen is supplied with a stove, fridge, and cabinets.
